The Book of Apotheosis

Through Truth. Into Character. In Service to Others.

The Book of Apotheosis begins with a confession: when Michael Silberman chose the name, he wanted to become God. Writing the book changed that ambition. The evidence led somewhere smaller, harder, and more honest — what began as a ladder toward divinity became a practice of letting reality correct the self.

This edition presents Apotheosis as a religion of conscious transformation, grounded in truth, character, and service. At its center are three responsibilities: the Duty to Become, the Right to Become, and the Right to Remain. The book applies a single, practical test to every claim of growth — the Test of What Remains: after the insight, the apology, or the crisis has passed, what survived in conduct?

Written for believers, skeptics, seekers, and anyone unwilling to choose between reverence and intellectual honesty.
