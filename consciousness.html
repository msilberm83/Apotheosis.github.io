Consciousness

The Mystery of Consciousness

One of humanity's greatest unanswered questions.

Consciousness is the one part of reality each of us knows from the inside. The brain builds working pictures of the world, other people, and the self, then revises them when reality no longer fits. Awareness matters because noticing “I am angry” or “I may be wrong about this person” creates enough distance to examine the picture instead of simply obeying it.

We still do not know why physical activity is accompanied by experience or how individual minds participate in divine consciousness. Apotheosis affirms Divine Reality as conscious while treating the structure of that consciousness as an open theological question, not a scientific result. What is already clear is ethical: every person is a center of experience whose inner life exceeds our picture of them. When evidence shows that picture is wrong, character is revealed by whether we let it change.
