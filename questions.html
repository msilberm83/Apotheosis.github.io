Honest Inquiry

Questions Protect the Path.

Honest faith does not fear an honest answer.

Whenever you encounter a claim, pause. Ask: What do we know? What do we reasonably infer? What remains possible? What remains unknown? These questions do not push God, mystery, or personal experience out of the conversation. They keep us from confusing the first story we build with reality itself. Then ask one more question: what could change my mind? A belief protected from every possible correction is no longer an honest conclusion. We protect wonder by refusing to fake certainty.
